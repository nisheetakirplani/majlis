What to do next

Also consider

  • Why: a decision-level question should end in a decision, not more reading.
  • One lead action keeps reading light; the rest stay one glance away.
  • "Closes" ties each action to the evidence, the same way each claim links to its source.
  • Risk: Approve implies Majlis can act. Until there is an integration, Approve should create a draft task, not send anything.
  • Test: do executives approve the lead action without opening the evidence? If so, the lead needs its "why" more visible.
